Stability research2025

Interaction-energy stability analysis

Built an analytical transient-stability framework for multi-GFM inverter systems under current limiting. A new inverter-to-inverter interaction-energy metric exposes how asymmetric tuning changes fault-induced angle stability margins.

02 / Conferences

Ideas shared in the room.

Conference talks, poster sessions, and research conversations that moved the work forward.

IEEE COMPEL · 2025

Oral presentation

Demystifying GFM large-signal stability with equivalent-circuit models

Presented an equivalent-circuit framework that shows where classical large-signal stability tools break down for grid-forming inverters. The work connects swing-equation intuition to inverter-specific dynamics, current limiting, and energy flow.

Read the conference note ↗
Austin, Texas · 2025

Two paper presentations

IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting

Presented two works on transient stability of grid-forming inverters. One was selected for the Best Paper Session; together they examine the Equal Area Criterion and the effects of heterogeneous dynamics in multi-inverter systems.

See the presentation details ↗
Phoenix, Arizona · 2024

Poster presentation

Fault ride-through for cascaded inverters using mixed GFM–GFL control

Presented with Rahul Mallik a singular-perturbation-based controller and hybrid fault ride-through strategy for series-connected converters, designed to improve recovery and transient stability beyond virtual-impedance baselines.

Read the ECCE recap ↗
Golden, Colorado · 2024

Research presentation

UNIFI General Meeting at NREL

Shared ongoing work on the large-signal stability of current-constrained inverter-based resources. The meeting capped a summer of collaborative research aimed at translating analytical stability tools into practical grid-forming systems.

04 / About

Controls grounded in physics.

My work sits where control theory, power electronics, and the grid meet. I study the nonlinear behavior of grid-forming inverters under real constraints—especially current limiting—and turn those insights into control strategies that can be tested in simulation and hardware.

Alongside research, I contribute to the UNIFI grid-forming-inverter specifications effort, develop open-source educational material, and have reviewed more than 100 papers for IEEE journals and conferences.
